show: photo face=45 bg=light from=35x45mm size=2x2in on=square
text: It's square
say: First, it's square. Not the tall rectangle you use for most passports. Same width as height.
---
show: number value="200 to 900" unit="pixels on each side"
text: The pixel size
say: The upload is a JPEG between 200 and 900 pixels on each side. Bigger than 900 gets rejected.
---
show: number value="200" unit="KB at most"
text: The file size
say: And the file must be under 200 KB. A photo straight off a phone is usually several megabytes, so it has to be made smaller.
---
show: compare face=45 bg=white,light mark=cross,tick Bright white | Light, not white
text: The background
say: The background is plain and light coloured, but not bright white. Pale grey or off white works well.
---
show: list ✓ No border | ✓ Face about 80% | ✓ Plain, even light
text: The rest
say: No border around the photo, and your face fills about eighty percent of it. Keep the light plain and even.

say: Part two, taking it at home. You don't need a studio. You need a wall, a window and a phone.
---
show: list 1. Plain light wall | 2. Stand a step in front of it | 3. Face a window
text: Set it up
say: Find a plain, light wall. Stand a step in front of it, so your shadow doesn't land on the wall. Then face a window, so daylight falls evenly on your face.
---
show: list ✓ Phone at eye level | ✓ About an arm's length away | ✓ Look straight at the lens
text: The camera
say: Hold the phone at eye level, about an arm's length away, or ask someone to help. Look straight at the lens.
---
show: list ✓ Mouth closed | ✓ Eyes open | ✓ Hair off your face
text: Your face
say: Keep a plain expression with your mouth closed, eyes open, and your hair away from your face.
---
show: photo face=47 bg=light size=2x2in mark=tick label="Square, light, centred"
text: Take a few
say: Take a few, then pick the sharpest one. Crop it square with your face in the middle.
