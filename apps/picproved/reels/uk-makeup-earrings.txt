title: Can you wear makeup or earrings in a passport photo?
description: Yes to both, for a UK passport. GOV.UK's photo rules don't ban make-up or jewellery: the photo must be a true likeness, with nothing covering your face and no reflections. What gets rejected is editing: beauty filters, skin smoothing and digitally enhanced faces. Big shiny earrings can catch the light, so pick small matt studs.
