title: Can you change your passport photo? (UK)
description: You can't swap the photo in a UK passport. GOV.UK says you need a new passport only if you can't be recognised from your photo any more, for example after plastic surgery, and then you need a countersignature. Dyed hair or a new beard is a slight change and needs no new passport. A new passport online costs £102, and it gets a new number, so don't book travel until it arrives.
